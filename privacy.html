Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026 · Updraft 1.0.0

The short version. Updraft has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your flights are stored on your phone and stay there unless you export them yourself. Nothing you record is sent to me, and I never see it.

What Updraft records

To do its job, Updraft reads the following from your phone while you are using it:

  • Location. GPS position, altitude and speed, used to record the track of your flight.
  • Barometer and motion sensors. Pressure altitude, acceleration and rotation, used for the altitude trace, the climb rate and the G reading.
  • Microphone. Only if you start an instructor debrief recording. Used to capture cockpit dialogue so it can be transcribed.
  • Speech recognition. Applied to that recorded audio to turn it into text for your debrief.
  • Photo library. Only when you choose to save an exported flight file or a debrief screenshot.

Where it goes

Onto your phone, and nowhere else. Flights, tracks, audio recordings and debriefs are written to a database on the device. They are not uploaded, backed up to me, or shared with anyone.

Audio from an instructor debrief is transcribed on the device using Apple's on-device speech recognition. The audio does not leave your phone, and it is not sent to Apple or to me for processing.

You can delete any flight, and its track, audio and debrief go with it.

What does leave your phone

Three things involve a network request, and it is worth being precise about them:

  • Map and terrain imagery. When you view your flight over 3D terrain or satellite imagery, the app requests map tiles for that area. The tile server necessarily sees the approximate area you are looking at, in the same way any map app works. It does not receive your flight, your track, or an identifier for you.
  • Weather forecasts. The soaring forecast requests conditions for a location from Open-Meteo. No account, no API key, no identifier.
  • Live airspace and traffic (Max tier, when enabled). Requests advisory airspace and transponder data for the area around you.

None of these requests carry a name, an account, an advertising identifier, or your stored flights.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. Updraft receives only whether a subscription is currently active — never your name, your payment details, or your Apple ID. Apple's own privacy policy governs the transaction.

What Updraft does not do

  • No user accounts and no sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties, because there is no data collected to sell.

Children

Updraft is rated 4+ and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. It has no account system and no way to identify a user.

Your control

Every permission Updraft asks for can be refused or revoked in iOS Settings, and the app degrades honestly rather than pretending. Refusing location means flights cannot be recorded; refusing the microphone means the debrief feature is unavailable. Nothing is silently worked around.

To remove everything, delete the app. All flights, tracks, audio and debriefs are stored inside it and go with it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
