Updraft is not a certified instrument. It shows ground speed, not airspeed, and its attitude display is a reference that needs calibration and says DRIFT when it can no longer be trusted. Do not fly on it. Look outside and follow your instructor.

Common questions

My flight was not detected as a launch

Updraft finds the launch from a sustained climb, which needs the barometer to be updating. If the trace looks flat, check that Updraft had motion permission and that the phone was not in a case that blocks the pressure port. You can always add the flight by hand: Logbook → Add by hand.

My altitude reads zero, or the barogram is flat

The phone's barometer is what gives Updraft real height. Some devices do not have one, and no simulator does. Recording still works from GPS, which is noisier — the Sensors tab tells you exactly which sensors this phone has and whether it can record properly.

The recording stopped when I put the phone in my pocket

iOS suspends an app in the background unless it has permission to keep using location. Allow location Always and Updraft will keep the trace alive with the screen off. Gaps are never drawn through — the report shows you exactly how much of the flight was recorded.

It says my flight had no climbs

Updraft only counts a climb as a thermal if you actually circled in it. A winch launch or an aerotow is not a thermal, so a training flight that towed up and glided down will honestly report no sustained climbs. That is the app being accurate, not broken.

How do I get my flights out?

Every flight exports as a standard IGC file, and the whole logbook exports as CSV. Both are yours, work in SeeYou and XCSoar, and need no account.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple and cancelled in your Apple Account settings: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. You can also reach Apple's sheet from Customize → Account and plan inside Updraft. Cancelling stops future renewals and you keep access until the period you paid for ends.

Do I need an account?

No. Recording, the logbook, reports, the drills and IGC export all work signed out and always will. An account adds backup, clubs, flight school and leaderboards.

How do I delete my account and data?

Customize → Account and plan → Account and sync → Delete cloud account. That removes the account and everything synced to it. Flights on the phone stay until you delete them in the logbook. Deleting the account does not cancel an Apple subscription — do that through Apple.

I found a number I do not believe

Please tell us, and say which flight. Every flight can export a full debug dump — open the flight → Export debug dump — which contains every fix and every reading. Attaching it to an email is the fastest way to get a wrong number fixed.

Reporting a bug

Useful things to include: your phone model and iOS version, what you expected, what happened, and the debug dump for the flight if it relates to one.
